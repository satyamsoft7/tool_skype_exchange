Corporate Terms, Privacy Policy & Compliance Architecture

Effective Date: October 2026 • Governing Standard: Zero-Access Client-Side Governance • Enterprise Support: support@skypeexchange4u.com

1. Enterprise Compliance & Security Architecture

The Zero-Server Privacy Guarantee: CloudTrim Enterprise operates under strict data minimization principles. All parsing of ActiveUsers.csv, Azure Disks, and tenant metrics occurs 100% within the user's local browser memory (RAM) via HTML5 File APIs. No tenant identifiers, user emails, or financial records are ever uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on external servers.

1.1 GDPR & Data Protection Regulation Alignment

  • Zero Cross-Border Data Transfers: Because directory datasets remain entirely within the administrator's local browser session, no international data transit occurs.
  • Right to Erasure (Instantaneous): All cached calculation objects and parsed tables are immediately destroyed the moment the browser tab is refreshed or closed.
  • No Cookies or Profiling: CloudTrim employs zero advertising cookies, third-party analytics pixels, or cross-site behavioral tracking scripts.

1.2 SOC-2 Trust Services Criteria Alignment (Security & Confidentiality)

  • No Service Account Ingestion: CloudTrim never requests, stores, or exchanges long-lived Azure Active Directory / Entra ID client secrets or certificate keys.
  • Read-Only Principle: Data collection via PowerShell scripts uses the least-privilege read-only scopes (User.Read.All, Directory.Read.All) without requiring tenant-wide modification permissions.

1.3 HIPAA & PCI-DSS Non-Applicability

CloudTrim is an administrative metadata calculator. The software does not store, ingest, or process electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) under HIPAA, nor does it touch or record payment card numbers under PCI-DSS. All payment transactions are offloaded to PayPal’s certified payment vaults.

2. Terms of Service

2.1 License Grant & Usage Rights

Upon payment of the applicable tier, CloudTrim grants the customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the remediation engine, executive PDF generator, and automated scheduler scripts:

  • Single Tenant Audit & Remediation Package ($39): Grants perpetual remediation script access and executive PDF export rights for one (1) designated Microsoft 365 / Azure tenant. Includes generation and re-runs of production-grade PowerShell remediation scripts for that specific tenant.
  • MSP Multi-Tenant Partner Tier: Grants Managed Service Providers rights to audit and produce co-branded governance deliverables across unlimited client tenants.

2.2 Independent Verification & Remediation Safety

CloudTrim provides estimated cost calculations and parameter-ready remediation scripts (e.g., Set-MgUserLicense, Remove-AzDisk). Administrators are explicitly advised to review all candidate accounts and utilize the provided Safe Dry-Run Mode (-WhatIf) prior to production execution. The operator maintains ultimate responsibility for tenant modifications.
