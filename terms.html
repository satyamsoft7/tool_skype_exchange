1. Technical Security Architecture & Data Minimization

The Zero-Server Privacy Guarantee: CloudTrim Enterprise operates under strict client-side data minimization. All parsing of ActiveUsers.csv, Azure Disks, and tenant metrics occurs 100% within your local web browser memory (RAM) via HTML5 File APIs. No tenant identifiers, user emails, or financial records are ever transmitted to, uploaded to, or stored on our servers.

1.1 Data Minimization by Design (GDPR & DPDP 2023)

  • Zero Cross-Border Data Transfers: Because directory datasets remain entirely within the administrator's local browser memory, no international server-to-server data transit occurs.
  • Instantaneous Erasure: All cached calculation objects and parsed tables are immediately destroyed the moment the browser tab is refreshed or closed.
  • No Persistent Credentials: CloudTrim never requests, stores, or exchanges long-lived Azure Active Directory / Entra ID client secrets, certificates, or OAuth tokens.
  • No Cookies or Profiling: CloudTrim employs zero advertising cookies, third-party analytics pixels, or cross-site behavioral tracking scripts.

1.2 Open Verification Principle

We do not claim third-party certification stamps where no independent external audit has been conducted. Instead, our security model is open and verifiable by inspection in your browser's developer tools: zero network transit when parsing files.

2. Terms of Service & Usage Rights

2.1 Community Pilot & Free Assessment Tier

CloudTrim is currently provided as a free community assessment tool during public beta. Users are granted a non-exclusive, revocable license to use the in-browser assessment engine, CSV validator, and generated PowerShell remediation scripts for internal evaluation across their Microsoft 365 and Azure environments without payment.

2.2 Administrator Due Diligence & Remediation Safety

CloudTrim calculates estimated waste and generates parameter-ready PowerShell remediation scripts (e.g., Set-MgUserLicense, Remove-AzDisk). Administrators are explicitly required to:

  • Review each candidate identity and resource individually prior to execution.
  • Verify whether litigation holds, in-place holds, or Microsoft Purview retention policies apply to shared mailboxes before removing licenses.
  • Confirm whether Azure Resource Locks exist before deleting unattached managed disks.
  • Always execute remediation scripts with the provided Safe Simulation Flag (-WhatIf) before executing live tenant changes.

The operator provides these scripts on an "as-is" advisory basis and maintains no access to or liability for administrative modifications made to your tenant.

2.3 No Online Automatic Billing

This website does not currently process credit cards or execute automated online subscription billing. If you require assisted enterprise audits or custom multi-tenant MSP automation, engagements are contracted through formal written statements of work.
